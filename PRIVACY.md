# PRIVACY (Public Alpha Scope)

## Scope

Public-alpha documentation and demo paths are designed to avoid exposure of private identifiers and secrets.

## Data handling expectations

- Use synthetic/demo data for examples.
- Do not embed personal or production identifiers in public examples.
- Keep local governance config (constitutionrules.yaml) private and uncommitted.

## Non-claims

- No claim that all possible integrations are privacy-reviewed.
- No claim that public-alpha examples represent full production governance coverage.
